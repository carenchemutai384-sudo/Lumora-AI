💡 Lumora AI

Illuminate Your Learning

Lumora AI is an AI-powered learning platform designed to help students understand their academic performance, plan their studies, track their progress, and receive personalized academic support.

I built Lumora AI from the idea that technology should not only tell students how they are performing, but also help them understand what they can do next.

🌐 Live Demo: https://carenchemutai384-sudo.github.io/Lumora-AI/

---

📖 About the Project

As a student, I became interested in how Artificial Intelligence could be used to make education more personalized.

Many students receive grades and academic results, but a grade alone does not always tell a student:

- Where they are struggling
- What they should prioritize
- How they should organize their study time
- Whether their current performance is improving
- What steps they can take to reach their goals

Lumora AI is my attempt to explore these questions through software.

The platform combines academic performance analysis, personalized study planning, AI-powered guidance, and progress tracking into one student-focused platform.

---

🚀 Project Evolution

Lumora AI has developed gradually from an initial idea into a more complete learning platform.

Rather than treating the first version as the finished product, I continued experimenting with the interface, functionality, and user experience as I learned more about software development.

Version 1.0 — The Beginning

The first version of Lumora AI focused on establishing the foundation of the project.

Initial goals

- Create a dedicated AI-powered learning platform
- Build a clean student-focused interface
- Establish the Lumora AI brand and visual identity
- Create the basic website structure
- Explore how AI could be incorporated into education

At this stage, the project was primarily about turning an idea into something that could actually be used.

---

Version 1.1 — Expanding the Learning Experience

As the project developed, I began moving beyond a simple landing page and started building separate experiences for different student needs.

The platform grew to include dedicated areas for:

- Academic performance prediction
- Student dashboards
- Study planning
- AI-powered academic assistance
- About and contact information
- Authentication pages

This was an important transition because Lumora was becoming more than a website—it was becoming a platform.

---

Version 1.5 — Introducing Student Performance Tools

One of the major directions of Lumora became academic performance analysis.

I developed a Student Performance Predictor designed to explore how machine learning could be used to estimate academic outcomes based on student information.

This introduced a more data-driven component to the project.

Instead of simply displaying information to students, Lumora began exploring the question:

«Can a student's existing academic information be used to provide useful predictions and guidance?»

This became one of the central ideas behind the project.

---

Version 2.0 — Building a Complete Platform

Lumora continued expanding into a more complete AI learning environment.

The platform developed features including:

📊 Performance Prediction

Students can provide academic information and use the performance prediction functionality to explore their expected academic outcomes.

📚 Personalized Study Planning

Lumora includes a Study Plan experience designed to help students organize their learning and create more structured study schedules.

🤖 AI Coach

The AI Coach provides an interactive space where students can ask questions and receive academic guidance.

📈 Progress Tracking

The dashboard provides a space for students to monitor their academic journey and visualize their progress.

🌙 Dark Mode

I also introduced dark mode to improve the overall user experience and give students more control over how they interact with the platform.

✨ Improved User Experience

As I continued developing Lumora, I worked on:

- Responsive layouts
- Navigation
- Visual consistency
- Animations and transitions
- Interactive components
- Mobile-friendly design
- Error handling
- Page-to-page consistency

🎯 Design Philosophy

Lumora AI is designed around a simple principle:

«Students should not only receive academic information; they should receive guidance that helps them act on it.»

The goal is therefore not to replace teachers or traditional learning.

Instead, Lumora explores how AI can become an additional learning companion—helping students understand their performance, organize their time, ask questions, and make more informed decisions about their learning.
